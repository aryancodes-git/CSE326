FAQs
How can I submit a query or complaint?
Select your user category, enter your Registration ID and contact email, choose an inquiry category, and describe your concern. Click "Send Message" to submit it.
Who can view my submitted complaint?
Your submission will be shared with the concerned department or authorized staff for review and resolution.
Can I visit the office in person?
Yes. You can visit the Academic Quality Assurance office during the listed working hours for assistance with your query.
