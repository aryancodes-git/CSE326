Faculty Feedback & Course Evaluation Portal

Your Feedback. Better Learning. Smarter Decisions.

Enhance the quality of education through a smarter and more organized feedback system. The Faculty Feedback & Course Evaluation Portal allows students to evaluate courses and faculty members through structured rating forms and anonymous feedback.

With secure student authentication, validated submissions, interactive reports, and an administrator dashboard, the platform transforms student feedback into meaningful insights that support continuous academic improvement.

Key Features:
  • Student Authentication
  • Anonymous Feedback
  • Interactive Reports and Analytics
  • Administrator Dashboard for Efficient Management
  • Validated Submissions for Accurate Data
