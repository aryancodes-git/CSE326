About the Faculty Feedback Portal
The Faculty Feedback Portal enables students to share confidential, anonymous academic evaluations that directly shape teaching excellence and course quality. Governed by the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC), every response is protected by secure cryptographic standards and guaranteed retaliation-free.
